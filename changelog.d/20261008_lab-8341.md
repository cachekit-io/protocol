### SaaS API — tighter post-delete bounds in the consistency contract

- [Consistency](spec/saas-api.md#consistency): after a project is deleted, its API keys stop working
  within about 40 seconds while the service is healthy, and a key idle at an edge location can be
  accepted for one more request up to about 5 minutes after the delete. The previous text allowed up
  to about 17 minutes.
- The same paragraph now says when cleanup happens: while the service is healthy, a cleanup that runs
  at least 30 minutes after the deletion normally removes, within 90 minutes of the deletion, data
  written in that window. The section stays informative and adds no SDK requirement.
