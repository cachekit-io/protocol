### Feature matrix — Rust's L1 refresh never waits on a held fill lock (LAB-8236)

- Note ¹⁰ of the **Stale-while-revalidate (client L1)** row no longer says Rust's refresh
  shares the cold-miss `single_flight()`. It takes the same locks but stands down at once
  when the key's in-process flight is held or its distributed fill lock is contested: no
  poll, no cache re-read, no PUT, and no miss counted, as
  [API-62](spec/saas-api.md#api-62) and [API-63](spec/saas-api.md#api-63) ask of a
  revalidation. Fixed in [cachekit-rs#147](https://github.com/cachekit-io/cachekit-rs/pull/147),
  unreleased.
