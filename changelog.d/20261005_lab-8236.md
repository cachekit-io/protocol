### Feature matrix — Rust's L1 refresh never waits on a held fill lock (LAB-8236)

- Note ¹⁰ of the **Stale-while-revalidate (client L1)** row now says Rust's refresh
  re-executes at most once. It also says what the published Rust crate (through 0.10.0)
  does:
  - on a contested fill lock, its refresh shares the cold-miss poll;
  - once its L1 copy has expired, it counts misses and then PUTs without the lease;
  - on a failed lock call, it fails open and recomputes.
- The note also records the unreleased fix in
  [cachekit-rs#147](https://github.com/cachekit-io/cachekit-rs/pull/147). The refresh stands
  down at once when the key's in-process flight is held, its distributed fill lock is
  contested, or its lock call fails. It does not poll, re-read the cache or PUT, and it
  counts no miss, as [API-62](spec/saas-api.md#api-62) and
  [API-63](spec/saas-api.md#api-63) ask of a revalidation.
