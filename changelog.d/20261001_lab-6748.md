### Intent presets — an interop cache has no legacy-decrypt (LAB-6748)

- [`spec/intent-presets.md` → Encryption Activation](spec/intent-presets.md#encryption-activation),
  migration story step 3: the `encryption=False` branch's obligation to keep
  legacy-decrypt from `CACHEKIT_MASTER_KEY` now holds for cachekit-py's auto-mode caches
  only, whose CK v3 frame header marks each encrypted entry. An interop entry carries no
  such header, and a reader that is not encrypting decodes stored bytes as plain
  MessagePack, so stale ciphertext is never decrypted: most stale entries fail to decode
  and are recomputed, and a rare one, most often a small one, is served as a wrong value.
  Turning encryption off in an interop cache therefore means moving the operation to a
  new namespace in every SDK that binds it, each of which also turns encryption off, in
  the same change; the branch points at cachekit-py's procedure for that.
- The migration story's opening "**MUST NOT** remove the ability to decrypt" and the
  closing "none strands the legacy-decrypt migration" are narrowed to CK-framed
  (cachekit-py auto-mode) caches to match, and rule 2's definition of legacy-decrypt
  points at the exception.
- The CK-framed obligation is unchanged. No wire, AAD or key-format change, and no
  conformance cell changes.
- **Affects interop caches that move from encryption (auto-activated or explicit) to
  `encryption=False`:** stale ciphertext is never decrypted. Move the operation to a new
  namespace in every SDK that binds it, each of which also turns encryption off, in the
  same change.
