### SaaS API — lifecycle times are whole seconds, stamped from `floor(now)`

- **Whole-second stamping is normative** ([Entry lifecycle](spec/saas-api.md#entry-lifecycle)).
  The store stamps a `PUT` from `floor(stored_at)` and a `PATCH /v1/cache/{key}/ttl` from
  `floor(now)`, so `fresh_until` and `evict_at` are always whole seconds on the store's
  clock. An entry is fresh for more than `ttl − 1` and at most `ttl` seconds after the write
  or `PATCH` that stamped it. The spec previously wrote these times as exact arithmetic (`stored_at + ttl`,
  `now + ttl`).
- The write-path tolerance, the re-serving-tier decay bound and the
  `GET /v1/cache/{key}/ttl` rounding now cite that rule. This documents the deployed
  server; no behaviour changes, and no SDK obligation moves.
