### SDK feature matrix — cachekit-rs 0.9.0 ships the empty-key rejection and `path-encoding.json` 1.1.0 (LAB-8738)

- [`sdk-feature-matrix.md`](sdk-feature-matrix.md): the Rust path-encoding cell gives the
  empty-key rejection as since `cachekit-rs` 0.9.0
  ([cachekit-rs#100](https://github.com/cachekit-io/cachekit-rs/pull/100)), no longer
  unreleased; `.`, `..`, `health`, `ttl` and `lock` stay since 0.8.0.
- The Rust "Test vectors in CI" cell gives
  [`test-vectors/path-encoding.json`](test-vectors/path-encoding.json) 1.1.0, vendored byte
  for byte (sha256-pinned) and CI-executed, as since 0.9.0. Both were checked on the
  published 0.9.0 `.crate`.
