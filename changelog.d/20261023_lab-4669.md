### Conformance — Rust meets Encryption Activation rule 2 from cachekit-rs 0.12.0

- [`spec/intent-presets.md`](spec/intent-presets.md#sdk-conformance): the Rust cell of
  "`CACHEKIT_MASTER_KEY` does not activate encryption on `minimal` / `production` / `io`" flips
  ❌ → ✅ `cachekit-rs` 0.12.0+
  ([cachekit-rs#173](https://github.com/cachekit-io/cachekit-rs/pull/173)).
  `CacheKit::from_env()` still reads and validates `CACHEKIT_MASTER_KEY` and
  `CACHEKIT_PREVIOUS_MASTER_KEYS` but no longer encrypts because the key is set. The new builder
  call `.encryption_from_env()` encrypts with those keys, tenant `"default"`, and is a
  `CachekitError::Config` when the key is unset or empty; the new `.no_encryption()` is the
  explicit opt-out (rule 3). From 0.12.0 (still in 0.13.0) a `from_env()` builder that saw the
  key and states no encryption intent (any encryption call, or `.no_encryption()`) fails at
  `build()`, a transitional gate like `cachekit` 0.21.0's one-release gate; design decision (B)
  records it. The default-tenant row adds that `.encryption_from_env()` derives under
  `"default"`, read against `default_tenant_interop` from 0.13.0. The rule 1 row lists
  `.encryption_from_env()` (0.12.0+) and `.encryption_with_previous()` (0.11.0+) among the
  builder encryption methods. Design decision (C) no longer says Rust carves out `from_env()`.
  The verification paragraph records the 0.11.0, 0.12.0 and 0.13.0 `.crate` inspections.
- [`sdk-feature-matrix.md`](sdk-feature-matrix.md):
  - the Rust key-rotation cell and note ⁵: previous keys are applied by `::secure_from_env`
    (0.9.0+) and `.encryption_from_env()` (0.12.0+), and by `from_env()` only through 0.11.x;
  - the Workers feature note: without `encryption`, `.encryption_from_env()` returns `Config`,
    and from 0.12.0 `from_env()` activates encryption in neither build, its `build()` gate
    applying with the feature on or off;
  - the backend-selection paragraph: from 0.12.0 `from_env()` validates the two key variables
    and applies neither;
  - the "`CACHEKIT_MASTER_KEY` auto-enables encryption" row and the preset-traps note: the key
    activates only `::secure_from_env` from 0.12.0 (`from_env()` too through 0.11.x), and they
    describe the transitional gate.
- [`spec/encryption.md`](spec/encryption.md#key-rotation-keyring) and
  [`decisions/key-rotation.md`](decisions/key-rotation.md) name `secure_from_env` and
  `.encryption_from_env()` as the Rust calls that apply `CACHEKIT_PREVIOUS_MASTER_KEYS`. No
  normative text changed.
