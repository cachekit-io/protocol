### Conformance — Rust meets Encryption Activation rule 2 from cachekit-rs 0.12.0

- [`spec/intent-presets.md`](spec/intent-presets.md#sdk-conformance): the Rust cell of
  "`CACHEKIT_MASTER_KEY` does not activate encryption on `minimal` / `production` / `io`" flips
  ❌ → ✅ `cachekit-rs` 0.12.0+ ([cachekit-rs#173](https://github.com/cachekit-io/cachekit-rs/pull/173)).
  `CacheKit::from_env()` still reads and validates `CACHEKIT_MASTER_KEY` and
  `CACHEKIT_PREVIOUS_MASTER_KEYS` but no longer encrypts because the key is set. The new builder call
  `.encryption_from_env()` encrypts with those keys, tenant `"default"`, and is a
  `CachekitError::Config` when the key is unset or empty; the new `.no_encryption()` is the explicit
  opt-out (rule 3). From 0.12.0 (still in 0.13.0) a `from_env()` builder that saw the key and
  states neither fails at `build()`, a transitional gate like `cachekit` 0.21.0's one-release gate;
  design decision (B) records it. The default-tenant row adds that `.encryption_from_env()` derives
  under `"default"` from 0.12.0. The rule 1 row lists `.encryption_from_env()`, and `.encryption_with_previous()` (0.11.0+, checked against the
  published 0.11.0 `.crate`), among the builder encryption methods. Design decision (C) no longer says
  Rust carves out `from_env()`.
- [`sdk-feature-matrix.md`](sdk-feature-matrix.md): the Rust key-rotation cell, note ⁵, the Workers
  feature note, the backend-selection paragraph, the "`CACHEKIT_MASTER_KEY` auto-enables encryption"
  row and the preset-traps note say that `from_env()` applies the key variables only through 0.11.x,
  describe the transitional gate, and say that a build without `encryption` returns `Config` from
  `.encryption_from_env()`.
- [`spec/encryption.md`](spec/encryption.md#key-rotation-keyring) and
  [`decisions/key-rotation.md`](decisions/key-rotation.md) name `secure_from_env` and
  `.encryption_from_env()` as the Rust calls that apply `CACHEKIT_PREVIOUS_MASTER_KEYS`. No normative
  text changed.
