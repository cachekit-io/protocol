### SDK feature matrix — Python runs the envelope pre-scan through cachekit-core 0.7.0 (LAB-6333)

- [`sdk-feature-matrix.md`](sdk-feature-matrix.md): the Python "Wire format (ByteStorage)"
  cell drops its envelope pre-scan and allocation-bound exceptions and its
  `reject_envelope_slots_overclaim` strict-xfail clause. cachekit-py pins cachekit-core
  0.7.0, which runs the [Retrieve Flow](spec/wire-format.md#retrieve-flow) step-2 pre-scan
  ([cachekit-py#618](https://github.com/cachekit-io/cachekit-py/pull/618)), and relies on
  core's allocation probe for the size-cap and ratio vectors' [WIRE-17](spec/wire-format.md#wire-17)
  bound, under the passthrough rule in [Reject vectors](spec/wire-format.md#reject-vectors)
  ([cachekit-py#627](https://github.com/cachekit-io/cachekit-py/pull/627)). Both are unreleased;
  cachekit-py 0.24.1 and earlier pin core 0.6.0 or older.
- The Python "Test vectors in CI" cell drops its envelope-entry-point exception: every
  `decode-bounds.json` 1.2.0 vector runs through `ByteStorage.retrieve`
  ([cachekit-py#627](https://github.com/cachekit-io/cachekit-py/pull/627), unreleased).
  Footnote 16 says the same, and that cachekit-py relies on core 0.7.0's allocation probe.
- The Rust (canonical `cachekit-core`) Wire format cell records that the pre-scan is on
  crates.io since 0.7.0.
- The two Python cells and footnote 16 mark
  [cachekit-py#522](https://github.com/cachekit-io/cachekit-py/pull/522),
  [#537](https://github.com/cachekit-io/cachekit-py/pull/537),
  [#550](https://github.com/cachekit-io/cachekit-py/pull/550) and
  [#561](https://github.com/cachekit-io/cachekit-py/pull/561) as released in cachekit-py
  0.23.0 where they said unreleased. The cachekit-rs and cachekit-ts cells and markers are
  unchanged.
- The Python "Test vectors in CI" cell and footnote 16 no longer say cachekit-py vendors the
  current `python-frame.json`: it vendors the
  [protocol#180](https://github.com/cachekit-io/protocol/pull/180) revision, without the
  `padded_header_frame` vector that [protocol#193](https://github.com/cachekit-io/protocol/pull/193)
  added.
