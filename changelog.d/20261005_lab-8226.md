### Test vectors — master key input rows in encryption.json (LAB-8226)

- [`test-vectors/encryption.json`](test-vectors/encryption.json) `1.3.0` adds a `master_key_input`
  block for [intent-presets.md → Master Key Input](spec/intent-presets.md#master-key-input). Every
  row comes from one key, under the tenant `"default"`.
  - **One accept row.** Its key has a leading zero byte, 14 bytes above `7f`, and every hex digit in
    both places of a byte. The row carries its derived-key fingerprint and a sealed interop entry, so
    a test can read it through a `secure` preset given no tenant.
  - **Nine hex rejects:** 65 digits, 63 digits, a last digit that is not hex, 64 digits followed by
    `gg`, a leading `+`, 31, 24 and 16 bytes, and 31 bytes with CR LF after them or `0x` before them.
  - **Three raw-key rejects:** 31 and 33 bytes, and the 64 ASCII bytes of a hex string.

  No existing vector changed. No row's verdict turns on an uppercase letter, white space or a `0x`
  prefix, which rule 1 does not settle. Every row also gets the same verdict whether an SDK takes
  exactly 32 bytes or 32 and more.
- [`tools/encryption-verify.py`](tools/encryption-verify.py) verifies the rows. It models each mistake
  a row's note names as a wrong entry point that must misjudge that row. Examples: a big-integer
  decoder that drops a leading zero byte; a signed-byte pair parser; key bytes passed through a text
  string; Node's `Buffer.from` ahead of a length check; an integer parser that takes a sign or a `0x`
  prefix; a length check made on the string before white space is skipped; an off-by-one or AES-size
  length rule; a raw-bytes path that takes 32 bytes or more. It also refuses any vector table it
  does not verify. [`tools/test_encryption_verify.py`](tools/test_encryption_verify.py) adds a
  mutation case for each guard.
- [`spec/intent-presets.md`](spec/intent-presets.md#design-decisions): the "No test vectors" design
  decision now says that Master Key Input's rules have vectors, that the rest of the specification
  has no fixture of its own, and why. No normative text changed.
- [`conformance/coverage.md`](conformance/coverage.md):
  - PRE-16 to PRE-18, PRE-22, PRE-23, PRE-26, PRE-51 and PRE-52 list the new rows and have narrower
    gaps. PRE-23 and PRE-52 move from gap only to vectors and a gap.
  - PRE-25, PRE-28 and ENC-6 name their sealed vectors instead of the whole file, and ENC-1's gap now
    speaks of every sealed vector.
  - PRE-20, PRE-21, PRE-33, PRE-45, PRE-46 and PRE-49 now name the construction case no vector holds,
    since the reject rows are refused at construction in some SDKs.
