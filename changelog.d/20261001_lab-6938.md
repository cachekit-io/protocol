### Tooling — stdlib python-frame verify FAILs malformed-JSON and non-UTF-8 frame headers (LAB-6938)

- `tools/python-frame-reference.py verify` FAILs a frame whose header bytes are not
  UTF-8, or are not valid JSON, as `FAIL <name>: parse error: header is not UTF-8: …` or
  `… parse error: header is not valid JSON: …`. It previously raised a traceback instead
  of reporting the vector, and an error vector carrying such a header crashed verify
  rather than counting as rejected.
- `tools/frame-crosscheck.mjs` (Node) is unchanged.
- `test-vectors/python-frame.json` is unchanged.
