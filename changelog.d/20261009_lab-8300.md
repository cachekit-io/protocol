### Conformance — cachekit-rs runs encryption.json 1.5.0 and the current python-frame.json (LAB-8300)

- [`conformance/sdks.json`](conformance/sdks.json) reads cachekit-rs at `3a0d241`, which vendors
  `encryption.json` `1.5.0`, recorded for the first time, and re-vendors `python-frame.json`, both
  byte for byte ([cachekit-rs#160](https://github.com/cachekit-io/cachekit-rs/pull/160)). The
  other five copies are unchanged. In [`conformance/coverage.md`](conformance/coverage.md),
  cachekit-rs's `ENC-1`, `ENC-2`, `ENC-3`, `ENC-7`, `ENC-9`, `PRE-19`, `PRE-23`, `PRE-27`, `PRE-29`
  and `PRE-51` move from `uncovered` to `partial (gap)`, and `ENC-5`, `ENC-6`, `ENC-10`, `PRE-16`
  to `PRE-18`, `PRE-22`, `PRE-25`, `PRE-26`, `PRE-28`, `PRE-31`, `PRE-32`, `IOP-19` and `IOP-20`
  from `partial` (no `encryption.json` recorded, or the previous `python-frame.json`) to
  `partial (gap)`.
- [Feature matrix](sdk-feature-matrix.md#protocol-compliance) **Test vectors in CI** row records
  cachekit-rs's `encryption.json` copy (`1.3.0` in
  [cachekit-rs#150](https://github.com/cachekit-io/cachekit-rs/pull/150), `1.5.0` in
  [cachekit-rs#160](https://github.com/cachekit-io/cachekit-rs/pull/160), unreleased) and the 29
  CK-prefixed `python-frame.json` vectors it refuses. Note ¹⁶ gives this repo's `encryption.json`
  as `1.5.0`, not `1.3.0`.
