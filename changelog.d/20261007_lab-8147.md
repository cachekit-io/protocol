### Test vectors — wire-format.json 1.4.0, file-backend.json 1.2.0 and python-frame.json close conformance gaps (LAB-8147)

- [`test-vectors/wire-format.json`](test-vectors/wire-format.json) `1.4.0`. No existing vector changed.
  - **Two vector pairs** on the `bin8` → `bin16` edge: `width_boundary_bin8_max` and `width_boundary_bin16_min`,
    whose `compressed_data` is 255 and 256 bytes. They are the first 253 and 254 bytes of
    `width_boundary_bin16`'s input, and both cachekit-core's `lz4_flex` and liblz4 emit their blocks.
  - **Three constructed vectors** on the `bin16` → `bin32` edge: `envelope_bin16_max` (65,535 bytes),
    `envelope_bin32_min` (65,536) and `envelope_legacy_array32_min`, the latter's fields as a legacy `array32`.
    Their block is not compressor output, so a writer is checked against them through an encoder given
    the fields.
  - **Eight reject vectors.** Five break step 2's typed decode: a fifth element, a missing fourth, a
    checksum of 9 and of 7 integers, and a legacy element written as 360; each passes a reader that casts
    instead of rejecting. `reject_envelope_slots_overclaim` breaks step 2's pre-scan by one slot, with every
    header fitting what follows it. `reject_original_size_sign_bit` (2⁶³ + 16) reads as negative in an
    `i64`. `reject_ratio_float32_rounds` is one byte past 1000:1 at 32,768 bytes of `compressed_data`,
    where a float32 ratio reads exactly 1000. Step-2 vectors carry `reject_check`, naming the check that
    rejects them.
  - **`payload_reject_vectors`**: two envelopes that every Retrieve Flow check accepts, whose payload is a
    `decode-bounds.json` reject document, for the payload decode after unwrapping
    ([Payload decode bounds](spec/wire-format.md#payload-decode-bounds)).
  - **`temporal_sentinel_vectors`**: the `__datetime__`, `__date__` and `__time__` maps, each with the value
    an SDK revives it as ([Datetime Extension Format](spec/wire-format.md#datetime-extension-format)).
  - **For SDK test harnesses:** a harness that iterates `reject_vectors` meets the new step-2 entries,
    which assert a typed-decode or pre-scan error (the [Reject vectors](spec/wire-format.md#reject-vectors)
    table's last column), and an `envelope_encoding` of `int-array` on `reject_legacy_element_above_255`.
- `tools/wire-format-reference.py`'s reader runs step 2's pre-scan, the header walk of
  `tools/decode-bounds-reference.py`. `tools/test_wire_format_reference.py` adds a mutant for the pre-scan,
  for each lenient cast the typed decode forbids, for a sign-reinterpreting `original_size` decode, and for a
  float32 or truncating ratio, each changing only its own vectors.
- [`test-vectors/file-backend.json`](test-vectors/file-backend.json) `1.2.0`: an empty and a binary payload
  (every byte value), flag bit 15, reserved values 1 and 255, and an expired entry with an unknown flag and
  one with a nonzero reserved byte, which a reader keeps: `miss_preserve`, never `miss_expired`.
- [`test-vectors/python-frame.json`](test-vectors/python-frame.json): every CK error vector names the check
  that rejects it (`rejected_by`). New error vectors sit one step from their check, on the default write's
  real header: a 6-byte frame, versions 2 and 4, and a header length one past the bytes present. Two more are
  other SDKs' containers, a bare envelope and plain MessagePack, which cachekit-py refuses. A new
  `encrypted_read_vectors` group, with its `encrypted_reader`, holds frames a cache configured for
  encryption fails closed on: two forged plaintext frames and two ciphertexts that do not authenticate
  under the reader's key or tenant. `tools/python-frame-reference.py generate` proves each against
  cachekit-py, and `verify` pins the group's set and each frame's sha256 to the proven bytes; `tools/test_python_frame_reference.py` adds a loosened parser per boundary.
- [`spec/wire-format.md`](spec/wire-format.md) describes the new vectors and groups. No normative text
  changed. [`sdk-feature-matrix.md`](sdk-feature-matrix.md) gains a temporal-sentinel row: cachekit-ts and
  cachekit-rs do not revive the maps in auto mode.
- [`conformance/coverage.md`](conformance/coverage.md): WIRE-2 and FILE-2 drop their gaps. WIRE-1, WIRE-5,
  WIRE-7 to WIRE-10, WIRE-12, WIRE-14, WIRE-20 to WIRE-24, FILE-1, FILE-3, KEY-3 and KEY-5 list the new
  vectors with narrower gaps, which name what stays open and why; WIRE-3, WIRE-4, WIRE-16 and FILE-4 list
  them under their existing gaps, and WIRE-13's gap no longer calls its vector the only `bin32`. They read
  partial in every SDK until it re-vendors.
