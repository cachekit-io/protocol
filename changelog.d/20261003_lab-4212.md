### Wire format — the xxHash3-64 checksum covers the payload only (LAB-4212)

- [`spec/wire-format.md` → Checksum: xxHash3-64](spec/wire-format.md#checksum-xxhash3-64)
  now states that the digest's input is the uncompressed payload only. `original_size` is
  outside it but cross-checked at step 8 of the Verification Flow; `format` is outside it
  and checked by no step.
- A non-normative note records the known limit: a rotted `format` passes the checksum.
- Clarification only: no byte, field, step order or reader behaviour changed.
