### Feature matrix — Python's `cachekit` 0.9.0–0.22.0 sniffs the plaintext on an `auto` config-drift read (LAB-8646)

- The Encryption section and the Python **AAD v0x03** cell of Compliance Status record a
  known violation of [ENC-2](spec/encryption.md#enc-2) and
  [ENC-3](spec/encryption.md#enc-3): in `cachekit` 0.9.0 through 0.22.0, a handler built
  with `serializer="auto"` (or its alias `"pythonic"`) and encryption off, given an
  encrypted entry whose stored header names that serializer, decrypts it with its master
  key and lets `AutoSerializer` sniff the plaintext. `main` refuses `AutoSerializer` in
  `EncryptionWrapper` from
  [cachekit-py#521](https://github.com/cachekit-io/cachekit-py/pull/521), unreleased.
