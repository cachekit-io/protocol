### Conformance — intent-presets requirement ids

- **Requirement ids**: every MUST and MUST NOT in [intent-presets.md](spec/intent-presets.md)
  (`PRE-1` to `PRE-55`) now carries an id, indexed in
  [conformance/requirements.json](conformance/requirements.json) and reported per SDK in
  [conformance/coverage.md](conformance/coverage.md). No normative text changed;
  `tools/conformance.py strip` removes the ids. Six keywords name the word rather than state
  a rule, so they are marked not-a-requirement: the subtitle's "what a preset name MUST
  configure", the sentence that says the keywords are used as in RFC 2119, and the three in
  the integrity sections that explain why the spec sets no MUST there.
- **What the new mappings show**: the spec has no fixture of its own, by design, so 38 of
  the 55 map to nothing and the other 17 borrow vectors, each with a gap. The hex master-key
  rules map to every vector that derives a key from one (`interop-mode.json`'s
  `interop_encryption_roundtrip` and `encryption.json`); every such key is exactly 32 bytes
  of well-formed, all-digit hex that repeats one nonzero byte, so a decoder that mishandles
  a hex letter, its case or a leading zero byte passes. The default-tenant rules map to
  `encryption.json`'s `default_tenant_interop`. The activation rules map to stored entries a
  test can plant: the two encrypted interop entries for "every operation encrypts", and the
  plaintext interop entry `issue_example_object` for "the master key's presence must not
  turn encryption on".
  None of them reaches a preset unless an SDK's test drives it through one. All 55 bind
  SDKs; 17 bind only the SDKs with the feature they govern: cachekit-py's migration rules
  and SDK-local presets, cachekit-rs's environment constructors and checksum-less
  container, the raw-bytes key rule (cachekit-py and cachekit-rs) and the `encrypted` alias
  ban (cachekit-py and cachekit-ts). No vector holds a key to reject, a preset's TTL or
  posture, a construction error, or what an SDK writes or keeps in L1.
