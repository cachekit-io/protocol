### Feature matrix — Python's `EncryptionWrapper` sniffs the plaintext with `AutoSerializer` in `cachekit` 0.1.0–0.22.0 (LAB-8678)

- The Encryption section and the Python **AAD v0x03** cell of Compliance Status record a
  known violation of [ENC-2](spec/encryption.md#enc-2) and
  [ENC-3](spec/encryption.md#enc-3): in `cachekit` 0.1.0 through 0.22.0, an
  `EncryptionWrapper` built with `serializer=AutoSerializer()` decrypts and then lets
  `AutoSerializer` sniff the plaintext. `main` refuses `AutoSerializer` in
  `EncryptionWrapper` from
  [cachekit-py#521](https://github.com/cachekit-io/cachekit-py/pull/521), unreleased.
- In 0.1.0 through 0.4.0 the wrapper defaulted to `AutoSerializer`, so the default
  constructor and every encrypted read by a cache handler built with `encryption=True`
  sniff the plaintext too, whatever serializer the handler is configured with. From
  0.6.1, the first PyPI release after 0.4.0, neither does.
