### Tooling — stdlib python-frame verify: type-strict original_size, serializer name required (LAB-6873)

- `tools/python-frame-reference.py verify` FAILs a vector whose
  `payload_envelope.original_size` is not a JSON integer, such as `32.0` or `true`. Before,
  Python's `32.0 == 32` and `True == 1` let them pass, although a typed reader that reads
  the field as an unsigned integer rejects them. The other declared envelope fields are
  compared type-strictly too, and so is the `twin_of` compare of the envelope fields, so
  `generate` warns when a twin's `original_size` is `32.0` against its base's `32`.
- `verify` FAILs a frame vector whose header records no serializer name: `s` missing, or
  not a non-empty string. [`spec/cache-key-format.md`](spec/cache-key-format.md) makes such
  an entry a mismatch, and cachekit-py's reader rejects it. Before, a vector whose
  `expected_header` also lacked `s` passed both the stdlib and the Node leg.
- The mutation suite now covers the `LZ4 decompress:` FAIL path, using a truncated block in
  an otherwise consistent vector.
- `test-vectors/python-frame.json` is unchanged.
