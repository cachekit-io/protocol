### Tooling — stdlib python-frame verify compares original_size type-strictly (LAB-6873)

- `tools/python-frame-reference.py verify` FAILs a vector whose
  `payload_envelope.original_size` is not a JSON integer, such as `32.0` or `true`. Before,
  Python's `32.0 == 32` and `True == 1` let them pass, although a typed reader that reads
  the field as an unsigned integer rejects them. The other declared envelope fields are
  compared type-strictly too.
- The mutation suite now covers the `LZ4 decompress:` FAIL path, using a truncated block in
  an otherwise consistent vector.
- `test-vectors/python-frame.json` is unchanged.
