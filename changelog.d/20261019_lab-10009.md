### SDK feature matrix — Python zero-config L1 and the `key=` key shape (LAB-10009)

- [`sdk-feature-matrix.md`](sdk-feature-matrix.md): the Python "Key-prefix shape
  (caller-supplied key)" cell was N/A. It now records the decorator's `key=` key,
  `{id}-{namespace or default}:{key}` from `cachekit` 0.24.0
  ([cachekit-py#583](https://github.com/cachekit-io/cachekit-py/pull/583)), and the
  `{namespace or default}:{key}` shape of 0.23.0 and earlier.
- The backend auto-detection note and the Python architecture notes no longer say that
  nothing configured falls back to localhost Redis. From `cachekit` 0.23.0
  ([cachekit-py#580](https://github.com/cachekit-io/cachekit-py/pull/580)) a plaintext,
  non-interop decorator caches in L1 only; encrypted and `interop=` decorators still fall
  back. Rust and TypeScript are unchanged: they require a backend.
