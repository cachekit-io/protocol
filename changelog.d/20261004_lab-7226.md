### Interop v2 — pre-check input copies bounded by allocated capacity (LAB-7226)

- [`spec/interop-v2.md` → Reader Algorithm](spec/interop-v2.md#check-order) now bounds an
  input copy made before step 4 by its allocated capacity: until step 4 passes, the
  capacity of each copy MUST NOT exceed the length of the bytes it is copied or decrypted
  from. The old text said a copy must be "no larger than" its source without saying
  whether that meant capacity or length, and forbade a copy from growing at all, which
  also ruled out filling one in pieces within that bound.
- The output-buffer examples no longer list "a copy of the payload for `method 0`", which
  could be read as the same buffer as the `method 0` payload copy the paragraph permits.
- No vector changed. interop/v2 is a draft that no SDK implements, so no conformance cell
  changes.
