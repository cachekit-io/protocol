### Feature matrix — Python's `cachekit` 0.22.0 accepts bare Arrow IPC after a decrypt (LAB-8601)

- The Encryption section records a known violation of
  [ENC-3](spec/encryption.md#enc-3): `cachekit` 0.22.0, the latest release on PyPI,
  returns the DataFrame from a decrypted bare-Arrow-IPC plaintext instead of hard-failing
  on the container mismatch. `main` refuses it from
  [cachekit-py#517](https://github.com/cachekit-io/cachekit-py/pull/517), which no release
  carries yet.
