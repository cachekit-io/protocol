### Feature matrix — Python's `cachekit` 0.9.0–0.22.0 accepts bare Arrow IPC after a decrypt (LAB-8601)

- The Encryption section and the Python **AAD v0x03** cell of Compliance Status record a
  known violation of [ENC-2](spec/encryption.md#enc-2) and
  [ENC-3](spec/encryption.md#enc-3): `cachekit` 0.9.0 through 0.22.0 returns the DataFrame
  from a decrypted bare-Arrow-IPC plaintext instead of hard-failing on the container
  mismatch. `main` refuses it from
  [cachekit-py#517](https://github.com/cachekit-io/cachekit-py/pull/517), unreleased.
